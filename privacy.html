Legal Notice • Client Privacy

Attorney Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Reviewed: 2026 • JMC Estate Law

1. Introduction & Commitment to Confidentiality

At JMC Estate Law (“the Firm,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), we recognize that estate planning, living trust creation, and probate administration touch the most sensitive personal, family, and financial aspects of your life. As a dedicated solo legal practice, we hold client confidentiality and data security as foundational tenets of our professional identity.

This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, maintain, and safeguard personal information submitted through our website (jmcestatelaw.com), electronic mail, telephonic communications, and initial consultation inquiries.

Solemn Professional Commitment: We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize personal information. All prospective and established client communications are handled under the strict ethical standards governing the legal profession.

2. Information Collection

We collect personal information through two avenues: voluntary submissions by prospective or active clients, and standard technical data generated automatically during website navigation.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you complete our online consultation inquiry form, telephone our office, send an email, or retain our services, you may supply personally identifiable information, including:

  • Personal Contact Information: Full legal name, residential mailing address, email address, and telephone number.
  • Legal Matter Context: Information regarding the services you require (e.g., establishing a revocable living trust, drafting a pour-over will, executing advance healthcare directives, administering a probate estate, or modifying existing documents).
  • Family & Beneficiary Background: Non-sensitive details concerning family composition, marital status, or minor children shared during initial intake discussions.
Warning Regarding Sensitive Information: Please do not submit highly confidential or proprietary details—such as Social Security numbers, bank account or investment numbers, passwords, or detailed medical histories—through the unencrypted web inquiry form. Secure, encrypted client portal transmission channels are provided once representation is evaluated or established.

B. Information Automatically Collected

When you browse our website, our web servers automatically log standard, non-identifying technical data necessary for server diagnostics and cybersecurity, which may include:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address (used strictly for network defense and geolocation diagnostics).
  • Browser type, browser version, and operating system.
  • Referring webpage URLs, pages visited within our site, and access timestamps.

3. Use of Information

Information collected by JMC Estate Law is utilized exclusively for legitimate legal practice management and client representation, including:

  • Prompt Evaluation of Legal Inquiries: Reviewing your inquiry to determine how we can assist your family, honoring our 1-business-day response guarantee.
  • Mandatory Conflict of Interest Screenings: Performing ethical checks in accordance with state bar professional conduct rules to ensure we do not represent parties with adverse interests.
  • Delivering Legal Services: Preparing bespoke estate planning portfolios, drafting trusts and deeds, communicating with probate court clerks, and executing legal agreements upon formal retention.
  • Administrative Operations: Coordinating consultations, providing calendar reminders, transmitting billing statements, and managing correspondence.
  • Statutory Compliance: Fulfilling recordkeeping requirements mandated by state bar associations, judiciary rules, and tax regulations.

4. Confidentiality of Inquiries & Ethical Duties

As a licensed solo attorney, Jeffrey M. Cohen, Esq. is bound by the highest ethical canons, including Rule 1.6 of the Rules of Professional Conduct (Confidentiality of Information) and Rule 1.18 (Duties to Prospective Clients).

Even before formal legal representation is commenced, we treat all factual disclosures submitted by prospective clients seeking legal counsel with strict professional confidence. We do not disclose prospective client discussions, personal family dynamics, or asset structures to third parties, except as expressly authorized by you or strictly required by applicable court order or ethical obligation.

5. Attorney-Client Privilege Notice

Important Legal Notice Regarding Formation of Representation: Visiting this website, reviewing educational articles, sending an email, or submitting an online consultation form does NOT create an attorney-client relationship between you and JMC Estate Law.

An attorney-client relationship is created solely when both you and JMC Estate Law execute a mutual, written Legal Representation Agreement that defines the explicit scope of work, legal responsibilities, and agreed fee arrangement.

While prospective inquiries are safeguarded under our ethical duty of confidentiality as outlined in Section 4, statutory attorney-client evidentiary privilege generally attaches only to confidential communications made for the purpose of obtaining formal legal advice once representation is established or prospective consultation is mutually agreed upon. Accordingly, please refrain from disclosing sensitive litigation facts or potential liabilities before conflict clearance is completed.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

JMC Estate Law does not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, behavioral ad pixels (such as Meta Pixel or Google Ads tracking), or third-party data broker analytics.

Our website utilizes only essential first-party cookies necessary for core website functions, including:

  • Security Tokens: Preventing automated bot spam submissions and protecting against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).
  • Session State: Remembering display preferences and ensuring smooth navigation between pages.

You can configure your internet browser to refuse all cookies or notify you when a cookie is placed. Doing so will not restrict your access to any legal information, guides, or contact forms on our website.

7. Third-Party Service Providers & External Links

To provide dependable, secure technology infrastructure, we partner with select, enterprise-grade service providers who are contractually bound to maintain strict confidentiality and data protection standards. These providers include:

  • Secure web hosting and domain infrastructure providers.
  • Encrypted business email and telecommunications platforms.
  • Legal practice management software compliant with bar security guidelines.

External Website Links: Our website may contain links to third-party resources, such as state probate court portals, statutory registries, or legal associations. JMC Estate Law exercises no control over the privacy practices, security standards, or policies of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

8. Data Security & Technical Safeguards

We implement comprehensive technical, administrative, and physical security measures designed to shield your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, destruction, or disclosure, including:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic to and from our website is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access Restrictions: Client files, consultation records, and communications are restricted solely to the attorney and authorized personnel under direct supervision.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): All firm systems, email accounts, and legal databases require multi-factor verification.

While we apply rigorous security precautions, no digital transmission over the internet or storage system can be guaranteed 100% impenetrable. If you prefer to discuss sensitive estate planning matters exclusively in person or by direct telephone, we are pleased to accommodate your preference.

9. Retention of Records

We retain client files and personal records only for the duration necessary to fulfill the legal representation, satisfy statutory record retention guidelines promulgated by the state bar, comply with financial audit rules, and protect against legal claims.

Consultation inquiries that do not result in formal legal representation are securely purged on a scheduled basis, with abbreviated conflict records retained solely to fulfill mandatory conflict-of-interest screening obligations under legal ethics rules.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Subject to applicable state bar ethics rules and legal retention requirements, you have the right to:

  • Inspect & Review: Request verification of the contact details we maintain regarding your inquiry.
  • Correct & Update: Request amendments or updates to inaccurate or outdated contact information.
  • Request Deletion: Request the deletion of prospective contact inquiries, provided retention is not required by bar regulations or ethical conflict checking.
